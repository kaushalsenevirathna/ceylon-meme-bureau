OUR STORY

The story behind the memes

Ceylon Meme Bureau began in 2023 as the Ceylon Meme Standardization Institute, a name that says a lot about how seriously we take Sri Lankan humour. Since then we have grown from a single Facebook page into a community of 10K+ followers across four platforms, and in 2026 we became the Ceylon Meme Bureau. Original memes, free templates and a community of creators: that is what we do.
