Submit Your Meme

Have a meme that deserves the spotlight? Share your original creation with Ceylon Meme Bureau for a chance to be featured across our official platforms.

Our team regularly reviews community submissions and selects the most creative, original, and entertaining memes to publish on our Facebook, Instagram, and website. Every featured submission receives full creator credit, including your name, social media profile, or page (as provided).

Before submitting, please ensure that:

  • Your meme is your original work or you have the rights to share it.
  • The content complies with our community standards and does not contain illegal, hateful, or harmful material.
  • Any social media username or page you wish us to credit is included in your submission.

Not every submission will be published, but every meme is carefully reviewed by our team. If your meme is selected, it will become part of the growing Ceylon Meme Bureau community while ensuring you receive the recognition you deserve.
